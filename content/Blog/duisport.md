## Abstract
With the expansion of the harbour of Duisburg, the debate over the quality of 
life in the neighbouring homes becomes more relevant. This paper aims to 
assess the environmental impact of the harbour of the Duisburger Hafen AG 
(called duisport). There will be a closer look at the specifics of the new Silk 
Road of the Belt and Road initiative and its impact. This is achieved by 
combining the results of different research which will be projected on the 
duisport. This will be opposed by the green projects, that the duisport has 
completed or raised interest in. This paper concludes that the duisport has a 
negative impact on the surrounding city, but duisport is investing in greener 
solutions and is trying to compensate for their negative impact. There could 
be done more for a greener environment, but this always must be feasible.
