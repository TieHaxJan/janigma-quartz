## Abstract
This paper is a literature review of temporal models, a class of AI systems designed to learn from time-series data. 
We synthesise findings on the main characteristics of temporal data, such as trends, seasonality, stationarity, and noise. 
We compare classical approaches (e.g., ARIMA) with more recent neural methods, including current architectures and time-aware transformers. 
The review identifies recurring challenges in the literature, including data quality and irregularity and concept drift. 
We also examine applications and emerging research directions, such as explainability, and federated and continual learning. 
By consolidating diverse strands of research, this review clarifies the current state of the field and points to opportunities for future work on temporal AI systems.
